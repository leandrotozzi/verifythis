## On Monday

#### *What to do with this once the screen goes off*

- **First thing: a testbench of your own, running.** The capstone is the
  minimum. If it is already handed in, the next one is a DUT of yours — the one
  at work, the one from your thesis, whichever. A finished course proves nothing;
  a testbench does
- **Keep two pages and throw away the rest:** the one-page cheat sheet and *The
  21 silent traps*. They are the two you open with the simulator running
- **If you get stuck, ask here:**
  [Discussions](https://github.com/leandrotozzi/verifythis/discussions) has one
  category per day. An example that does not run is a **course issue**
- **And if something confused you, fix it.** It is CC BY 4.0 and the whole repo
  is there: the slides, the examples, the graders and the linters. A PR that
  clears up a slide is worth as much as one that adds a section

*Thanks for getting this far.*

Note:
The last slide of the course, and it is worth it being short and in the
imperative: after seven days nobody takes away one more new idea, but they do
take away one instruction.
The instruction is the first line, and it is worth saying it looking at the
camera or at the group: **the only thing that proves somebody can do this is a
testbench of their own, running**. Not the capstone marked, not the fifteen rows
of the self-assessment in green: one of their own, on a DUT nobody prepared to be
easy.
The second bullet is the most practical advice on the slide and the one most
thanked six months later. Of everything that got printed this week, what gets
opened again is two pages: the cheat sheet —`docs/en/uvm-cheatsheet.pdf`, one
page, A4— and the silent-traps table, which is written to be searched by symptom
and not to be read.
And the invitation in the fourth is not courtesy. The course improves where
somebody trips: half the presenter notes exist because somebody asked the same
thing twice. If it is taught in class, close by explicitly asking for the three
things that were not understood — and writing them down before the group leaves.
