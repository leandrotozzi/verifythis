## Agenda

#### *Day 8 · ≈ 4 h · optional · what comes after the capstone*

- RAL, unit 9 — the register model over the same APB
- The reference model in C, through DPI
- The second capstone: a FIFO with backpressure

Note:
This day is **optional and it goes after the wrap-up**, and both things are on
purpose. The course ends on day 7: whoever got that far wrote a
complete UVM testbench from a blank sheet and lacks nothing to
work. What is here is what gets added once that has happened.
Why these three and not others: all three need the capstone to be already
done. RAL replaces the scoreboard the student has just written —and it is not
understood before having written it—; the golden model in C replaces the prediction
of the scoreboard, the same thing; and the second capstone only makes sense for whoever
has already handed in the first one, because what it teaches is **where the pattern of the
first one stops being any use**.
For whoever is teaching: in a company this is another half day, and it usually gets given
when the group has real registers in the project. In a semester it is
the optional unit, or the final project for those who want a high mark.
